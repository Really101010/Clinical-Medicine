#### Mechanical forces [机械应力](机械应力.md)
Mechanical forces, including <mark style="background-color: #1A4F10; color: white">stretch, shear and deformation</mark>, that cells experience in the profibrotic niche (defined here as the various inflammatory cytokines, activated cells, dysregulated ECM and other stimuli within the injury microenvironment) may also play an important role in the onset and progression of fibrosis.
[Tissue mechanics and fibrosis - ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925443913000525?via%3Dihub)：
- Whether the mechanical force originating at the cell boundary is transmitted directly to the nucleus or to nuclear proteins (Yap/Taz, for example) [[9]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925443913000525?via%3Dihub#bb0045), and whether signaling cascades are activated (potentially _via_ <span style="font-size: 16px; color: #D5E0A0">focal adhesion kinase (FAK)</span> or other focal adhesion proteins) as a result of tension at the site of the focal adhesion, are issues that have stimulated extensive investigation [[10]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925443913000525?via%3Dihub#bb0050).


> [!7.16 idea] 关于材料缝合
> 通过缝线造模肠[[粘连]]，是否不仅仅因为结扎血管造成缺氧，还有各个方向机械应力促进纤维化的原因？

1. <mark style="background-color: #3F4B5E; color: white">Fluid flow</mark>
2. hydrostatic pressure, osmotic pressure
	These forces may be related to flow (pulsatile flow, for example, causes stretch; elevated hydrostatic pressure results from obstructed flow; and interstitial flow is driven by gradients in hydrostatic and osmotic pressure). 
	Obstruction of the bile ducts, pancreatic duct, and ureters leads to fibrosis, likely due at least in part to changes in <mark style="background-color: #3F4B5E; color: white">shear stress (stasis) and hydrostatic pressure</mark> [[31]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925443913000525?via%3Dihub#bb0155). 
	The development of cardiac cirrhosis in response to high central venous pressures suggests that elevations in hydrostatic pressure are highly relevant to fibrosis _in vivo_. 
3. <mark style="background-color: #3F4B5E; color: white">Stretch</mark>: particularly important in the lung,[肺纤维化](肺纤维化.md) which is subject to cyclical stretch during respiration [[32]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925443913000525?via%3Dihub#bb0160).
