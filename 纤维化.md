#### Mechanical forces
Mechanical forces, including <mark style="background-color: #1A4F10; color: white">stretch, shear and deformation</mark>, that cells experience in the profibrotic niche (defined here as the various inflammatory cytokines, activated cells, dysregulated ECM and other stimuli within the injury microenvironment) may also play an important role in the onset and progression of fibrosis.
