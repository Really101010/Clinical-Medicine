Materials in FDM Composites

##### Polymer Matrix (Common)
- **PLA** – most common matrix for fibre‑reinforced FDM.
- Nylon (PA) – tough, often used with carbon fibre (Nylon+CF).
- ABS – also used, but less common than PLA/Nylon for fibre reinforcement.
- PVC – rarely used (poor printability, hazardous fumes).
##### Ceramic Filler
- **Alumina (Al₂O₃)** – common ceramic material added to polymer matrix for improved stiffness/heat resistance.
##### Reinforcement Fibres
- Carbon fibre, glass fibre – added to matrix to boost **Young’s modulus** and **tensile strength**.

| Aspect | Impact | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A. Voids / air gaps** | ✅ Affected | Controlled by infill density, pattern, and overlap. |
| **C. Mechanical properties** | ✅ Affected | Raster orientation creates **anisotropy** (X/Y > Z in strength & modulus). |
| **D. Surface quality** | ✅ Affected | Layer height, line width, wall loops determine roughness & stair‑stepping. |
| **B. Threaded hole reinforcement** | ❌ Not affected | Depends on **geometry design** (ribs/thicker walls) or **post‑processing** (inserts). |

[^1]: “ABS”在3D打印/塑料的背景下几乎普遍指丙烯腈-丁二烯-苯乙烯共聚物（Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene）
